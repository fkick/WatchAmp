Privacy Policy

WatchAmp privacy policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

Information We Collect

WatchAmp itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to NerdyPup or any third-party analytics services. All data used by the app stays on your device or communicates directly with your self-hosted Plex Media Server.

Plex Account Authentication

WatchAmp requires you to sign in with your Plex account to access your music library. During the sign-in process:

  • You authenticate directly with Plex's servers using a PIN-based flow.
  • WatchAmp receives an authentication token from Plex, which is stored locally on your device.
  • Your Plex username and password are never seen or stored by WatchAmp.

Local Data Storage

The following data is stored locally on your Apple Watch:

  • Plex authentication token used to communicate with your Plex Media Server.
  • Server connection details including the address of your Plex Media Server.
  • Downloaded music files for songs you choose to download for offline playback.
  • Play cache for recently played songs cached for faster playback.
  • App preferences such as transcode preferences, library selection, and playback options.

None of this data is transmitted to NerdyPup or any third party.

Third-Party Services

Plex

WatchAmp connects to your Plex Media Server and Plex's authentication services. Your use of Plex is subject to Plex's own privacy policy and terms of service. We encourage you to review Plex's policy at plex.tv/about/privacy-legal.

WatchAmp communicates with Plex services solely to:

  1. Authenticate your Plex account via Plex's PIN-based sign-in.
  2. Discover and connect to your Plex Media Server.
  3. Browse, stream, and download music from your personal Plex library.

WatchAmp does not access any Plex data beyond what is required to provide music playback functionality.

Apple

WatchAmp offers optional in-app subscriptions processed entirely through Apple's App Store. Subscription transactions are handled by Apple and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Review Apple's policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. WatchAmp does not include any advertising, analytics, or tracking frameworks.

Data Security

All communication between WatchAmp and your Plex Media Server uses HTTPS when available. Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device. Downloaded music files are stored in the app's local storage on your Apple Watch.

Children's Privacy

WatchAmp does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this privacy policy, please contact us at admin@nerdypup.com.
